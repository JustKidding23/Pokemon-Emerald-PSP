## The PC port: [NTx86/pokeemerald-sdl2pc](https://github.com/NTx86/pokeemerald-sdl2pc)

Made by [NTx86](https://github.com/NTx86), who first ran the decompilation as a PC program,
with [JaceCear](https://github.com/JaceCear), [BiffaloXIII](https://github.com/BiffaloXIII) and elyro1.

## [fuddlesworth/pokeemerald-native](https://github.com/fuddlesworth/pokeemerald-native)

[fuddlesworth](https://github.com/fuddlesworth) carried on the PC port: the sound engine
checked against the GBA's, controller support and a settings file, the headless tests and
macOS.

## The PlayStation 2 port: [dnllnoe/pokeemerald-ps2](https://github.com/dnllnoe/pokeemerald-ps2)

[dnllnoe](https://github.com/dnllnoe) made the game build for a 32-bit MIPS console and
drew it with the PS2's Graphics Synthesizer. This port's base.

## The PSP port

[JustKidding23](https://github.com/JustKidding23), with Claude (Anthropic's AI assistant)
in Claude Code, which wrote the code. See [How this was made](README.md#how-this-was-made).

## Tools

- [pspdev](https://github.com/pspdev): the PSP toolchain and pspsdk the game is built with
- [PSPLink](https://github.com/pspdev/psplinkusb), by TyRaNiD, Julian T, Rasmus B, John_K and
  pspdev: starting and debugging the game on a PSP over USB
- [PPSSPP](https://github.com/hrydgard/ppsspp): testing it on a PC
- [atracdenc](https://github.com/dcherednik/atracdenc), by Daniil Cherednik: the PSP menu's
  music
